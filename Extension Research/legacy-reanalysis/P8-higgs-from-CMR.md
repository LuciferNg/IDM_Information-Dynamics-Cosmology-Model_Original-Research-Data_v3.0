## CMR Reframe

The Higgs mass is 125 GeV. The Planck scale is ~10¹⁹ GeV. The hierarchy problem is: why 17 orders of magnitude?

**CMR approach:** The Higgs field is a coherent mode of the vacuum. Its W allocation determines its mass:

m_H = M_Planck * W_H

If W_H ≈ 10^-17 for the Higgs mode, then m_H ≈ 10¹⁹ * 10^-17 ≈ 100 GeV. ✓

**Why W_H ≈ 10^-17?** The Higgs is the least-accessed mode of the vacuum — it's only "used" for mass generation. Most vacuum modes have higher W (graviton, photon, etc.).
